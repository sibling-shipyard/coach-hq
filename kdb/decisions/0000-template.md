Enforces is the field to draft first. If you cannot name in one line what this ADR
stops someone doing, you do not yet have a decision — you have a description. The
four ADRs that invented this field before it existed (0023, 0024, 0027, 0031) are
also four of the clearest in the set, and that is not a coincidence: the sentence is
hard to write and writing it is what forces the thinking.

Why must be falsifiable. If it would still read true after the Decision was reversed,
it is a restatement, not a reason. No check catches this — it is what review is for.

Short sentences. One thought at a time. Over 40 words fails the build; the house voice
rules in platform/soul/A_identity.md §3 apply to us, not only to Coach.

Plain English — short words, no jargon. Someone outside the team should understand it.
Cite real paths in backticks so it is greppable; they do not count against you.

Length is not the enemy and short is not a virtue in itself: 0025 is one of the
longest ADRs here and the most readable, 0014 was one of the shortest and among the
worst. Write it short because you cut what does not matter, not to hit a number.
