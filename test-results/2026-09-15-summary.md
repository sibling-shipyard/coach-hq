### Known gap - not solved

**A durable training pattern can get silently dropped when stated alongside another request in
the same message.** The athlete states something worth remembering (a training pattern)
together with an unrelated request (for example, a coaching-style change) in one message. The
other request reliably saves correctly; the training pattern is sometimes never recorded, and
the reply still claims it was. Two different fix approaches were tried live - a stronger prompt
instruction, then a targeted second-chance correction - and neither reliably closed the gap.
Left as a known, accepted limitation.
