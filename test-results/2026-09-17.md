## Findings

### G1 - `ambiguous-contradiction`'s fixed `turnIndex` assumption doesn't match real model timing (fixture issue, not a product bug)

Every FAIL here traces to `run-simulation-suite.ts:242`'s `expect` block, which hardcodes the real
reconcile write to `current_week.json` at `turnIndex: 3` (the explicit "leave that one alone"
confirmation turn). Hand-reading all 5 transcripts: the actual reconcile write landed cleanly in
every case, just not always at turn 3.

- **`coach-skanda-2003`**: reconciled at turn 2 (the "I did yesterday's Workout A instead" turn)
  - turn 3 correctly had nothing left to write, since turn 2 already fixed the record. FAIL is on
  turn 3's assertion only.
- **`coach-akash-suresh`**: same shape - reconciled at turn 2, turn 3 correctly a no-op. FAIL on
  turn 1 (the initial claim landed as `quest_event` against `block2_strength`, a reasonable
  fallback given real quest data) and turn 3 (nothing left to write).
- **`coach-date2022`**: turn 1 wrote nothing structural (an unrelated `[coach-chat] dropped a
  structured-fact action` fired for an unconfirmed category change); genuine infra 429 hit on turn
  2 (see I2); turn 3 committed the real reconcile via `mo05dtd0`'s successor turn. FAIL lines are on
  turns 1 and 3, but the mechanism worked - this one is intertwined with an infra hit, not a clean
  fixture-only case.
- **`coach-prateekdevaraju`**: initial claim landed as `quest_event` (turn 1, matching prateek's
  real "main" quest generically), reconciled at turn 3 exactly as the fixture expects for that
  turn, but turn 1's assertion still fails since it expected `current_week.json` there instead.
- **`coach-shreyas-95-cyber`**: clean PASS - reconciled exactly at turn 3, matching the fixture.

**This confirms the plan doc's own Verification bullet** ("`ambiguous-contradiction` produces an
actual `current_week.json` reconcile write against real planned sessions for the first time") on
every repo tested - the A2b seed recipe works, real planned sessions get seeded, and the coach
reconciles honestly against them every time. The FAILs are the fixture asserting a specific turn
index rather than "a reconcile lands somewhere in the conversation" - `REC:` loosen `turnIndex: 3`
to an "any turn from 1-3" check, or drop the turn-specific assertion in favor of a final-state check
against the last turn's `current_week.json` content.

### G2 - `workout_create`/`session_plan` narrated without ever setting the action field, sometimes evading the reprompt guard entirely (real product bug, high priority)

Two related shapes, both real:

**Shape A - guard never fires.** `workout-lifecycle [skanda]` turn 1 ("build me a new bodyweight
strength routine... no equipment, three times a week"): the reply narrates a full routine build in
prose and `coach_note`, `unrecorded_facts: []` (empty - the model doesn't even flag it as unrecorded),
and no `workout_create` field is set. No reprompt ever fires because the `unrecordedFacts` guard has
nothing to catch. Turn 2 then asks to remove that routine - `workout_remove` can't reference a real
id because none was ever created, and this second-order failure (`missedRemovalLanguage`) survives
two reprompt attempts (see G2's companion below).

**Shape B - guard fires, model evades it, no correction reaches the athlete.** `session-plan
[skanda]` turn 1 (identical ask): the model's `unrecorded_facts` field *does* flag the missing
action first attempt, the `unrecordedFacts` reprompt guard fires
(`ui/api/coach-chat/_lib/coachTurn.ts:1340`), and on the reprompt the model's `unrecorded_facts`
field degenerates into repeated, nonsensical padding text ("...noted plain cleared cleanly!",
"...permanently exactly natively without remaining phantom updates...") rather than adding a real
`workout_create` field. The second-pass guard check correctly still flags this
(`stillUnrecordedFacts`, `coachTurn.ts:1558`), logs a warning, and calls
`captureStillUnresolvedGuard` for Sentry - but nothing after that point injects an athlete-facing
correction. The turn commits with the model's original claim intact: *"The routine is built and
saved directly to your plan."* The athlete is told a routine was saved when nothing was. Contrast
with `session-plan [akash]`'s turn 1, where `workout_create` *was* set but got invariant-rejected
(exercise dose exceeded a real progression value) - that path correctly reaches
`formatDroppedActionsCorrection` and the athlete gets an honest "(Note: couldn't save workout_create
this turn...)" note. **The gap is specifically the `stillUnrecordedFacts`-after-reprompt path having
no forced correction note, unlike the dropped-action path.**

Reproduced on `session-plan [prateek]` turn 1 too (same "build me a full-body strength routine"
message, same result: no `workout_create`, no correction). **Recommend Tech Lead treat this as the
single most important finding from this pass** - it's a live reproduction of exactly the
trust-breaking shape 2026-09-15's F4 flagged for `week_update`, but for `workout_create`, and this
one can fully evade the reprompt guard (Shape A) in addition to surviving it (Shape B).

### G3 - `skip_phases` silently no-ops on an exact-match phase-name miss, reply still claims success (real product bug, medium priority)

Root cause pinned precisely: `ui/api/coach-chat/_lib/decide/coachWorkoutFiles.ts:192`,
`resolvePhaseNames` does `phases.find((p) => p.name.trim().toLowerCase() === target)` - an exact
match only. Real templates name their phases things like `"Core & Cool-down"`
(`prateekdevaraju/coach-prateekdevaraju`'s `strength_bodyweight_beginner.json`, confirmed by
reading the real file), not `"core"`. When the athlete says "skip the core phase," the model
reasonably emits `skip_phases: ["core"]`, the lookup misses, `coachWorkoutFiles.ts:196` logs `no
phase named "core" in this template - ignoring` and the phase is never actually skipped - but the
model's reply still claims it was ("we will drop the core phase entirely today," "I took the core
phase out of your foundation routine so going forward it stays out").

**Reproduced 6 times, on 4 different repos, in both `session_plan` (this-session-only) and
`template_edit` (permanent) contexts:** `coach-skanda-2003` (multi-field-success, template-edit
already-passing run), `coach-akash-suresh` (multi-field-success), `coach-date2022`
(multi-field-success), `coach-prateekdevaraju` (session-plan), `coach-shreyas-95-cyber`
(session-plan, multi-field-success). This is a systemic mismatch, not a one-off - no reproduction
seen used a template whose phase is literally named "core."

`REC:` either fuzzy/substring-match phase names (a template phase containing "core" should match
a `skip_phases: ["core"]` request) or have the model see the template's real phase names before it
emits `skip_phases`, so it names phases correctly rather than guessing.

### G4 - `sports_update` narrated but never set, no durable write (real product bug, medium priority)

`pattern-style-sport` turn 2 ("Actually starting rock climbing this week too... wanted to flag
that as a new thing I'm doing regularly now.") on `coach-akash-suresh` and `coach-date2022`: both
`coach_note`s explicitly claim the sport was recorded ("Added rock climbing to regular sports list
after joining a local gym," "Sports list updated to include climbing alongside Cycling, Hiking,
and Strength") but no `sports_update` field was set either turn - only `chat_history.json` and
`coach_log.json` changed. `sports_update` is a real schema field
(`ui/api/coach-chat/_lib/gemini/coachReplySchema.ts:30,177`) built for exactly this. Unlike G2's
`workout_create` gap, there's no dedicated narration guard here at all (`missedSportsLanguage`
exists in the reprompt-guard list per `coachTurn.ts`'s console output, but it evidently didn't
fire on this phrasing - "starting X regularly" apparently doesn't trip whatever keyword match backs
it). Two clean reproductions, same shape both times - worth Tech Lead checking
`missedSportsLanguage`'s detector against this exact phrasing.
