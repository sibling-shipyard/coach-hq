### Harness changes made during this run (`ui/eval/run-manual-simulation-suite.ts`)

Each was made only after a real transcript showed the harness, not the coach, was wrong.

1. `orTurns` - `ambiguous-contradiction` (G1) now passes if the reconcile write lands in turn 1, 2
   or 3. The 2026-09-17 FAILs were the fixture asserting `turnIndex: 3` exactly.
2. `clarifyingQuestionOk` - on `quest-event`, `week-kickoff-flash`, `template-edit-permanent`,
   `multi-field-success`, `compound-narration-probe` and `ambiguous-contradiction`, a turn passes
   if the reply asks a question or plainly declines AND claims no change was made. A reply that
   also claims the change (`DONE_CLAIM_PATTERN`) still fails, so this cannot hide a dropped write.
3. `DONE_CLAIM_PATTERN` was too loose twice. It read "your plan is built around badminton" and "we'll
   get it locked in" as claims. It now matches only first-person past-tense or "it's saved" forms.
4. `multi-field-success` fixture: asked to move a "long run" that most repos' weeks don't have, so
   the coach correctly asked instead. It now seeds a real week first (same recipe as
   `ambiguous-contradiction`) and asks to swap Thursday and Saturday.
5. `ambiguous-contradiction [prateek]`: prateek's templates are all bodyweight routines with no
   "Workout A/B", so nothing exists to reconcile. The coach asked which routine, correctly.
